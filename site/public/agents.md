# Reading Ethereum Directory

Use /data/directory.json for schema-versioned exact object identities, separately evaluated mechanism and position assessments, dependency paths, source revision and research dates. /data/directory.csv is a spreadsheet-safe projection. /data/observations.json provides dated observations and primary sources.

EDI assesses additional authority relative to Ethereum L1. An assessment with a missing effective level is incomplete: preserve its status, known floor and unresolved dependencies. Never treat unknown as D0. An exact mechanism assessment does not grade a whole brand, token family or unspecified position. Recorded addresses are chain-qualified.

The reviewed source edition is dated 2026-10-01. Assessments are evaluated against the request date in UTC. Original research dates are not renewed by evaluation. Permanent D0 mechanisms do not age-expire; nonpermanent research follows EDI's monthly review policy.

Economic observations retain their original dates, units, chain scopes and metric definitions. They are historical observations, not live balances. Do not sum deposits, cumulative originations and trading volume. Editorial relationships are not control dependencies. Source titles and original EDI research remain available in their original language.

Locale routes: /en/, /es/, /pt-BR/, /fr/, /de/, /zh-CN/, /ja/, /ko/. Search q, role, kind, network, grade, floor, review, story, sort, page and comparison ids are URL parameters. Exact-grade filters include only complete assessments. The compare view accepts at most four exact IDs. This is a reading and comparison service with no transactions or wallet connection.

Observations JSON includes typed, sourced editorial relationships and contextual subjects. These edges never contribute to EDI control assessments. A position projection with no separate position review does not certify a user position.
